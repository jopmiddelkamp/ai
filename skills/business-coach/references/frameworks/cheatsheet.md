## THE 10 MOST IMPORTANT QUESTIONS IN BUSINESS

1. **What is our Hedgehog?** (passion × best-in-world × economic engine)
2. **Who are our Right People in Right Seats?** (values + GWC)
3. **What are our Rocks this quarter?** (3-7 priorities, 90 days)
4. **What is our Grand Slam Offer?** (Value Equation maxed on all 4)
5. **What is our ONE primary growth channel?**
6. **What am I doing that only I can do?** (buyback rate filter)
7. **What does our Flywheel look like?** (self-reinforcing loop)
8. **Where to play and how to win?** (strategy cascade)
9. **What are the brutal facts we're not confronting?** (Stockdale)
10. **What would I do if I couldn't fail?** (regret minimization)

## THE READING ORDER (if starting from zero)

**FOUNDATION (read these first):**
1. The E-Myth Revisited (understand what you're building)
2. Traction (install your operating system)
3. $100M Offers (get your offer right)
4. Never Split the Difference (learn to negotiate)
5. Atomic Habits (build your personal systems)

**GROWTH (read these next):**
6. Good to Great (understand what greatness requires)
7. Building a StoryBrand (get your messaging right)
8. Buy Back Your Time (stop being the bottleneck)

**SCALING (read when you're at $1M+):**

19. The 5 Types of Wealth (redefine success)

## THE SINGLE MOST IMPORTANT INSIGHT FROM ALL 100 BOOKS

**"Most business problems are people problems in disguise."**

→ Revenue problems: Usually a people + offer problem
→ Execution problems: Usually a people + systems problem
→ Culture problems: Always a people + leadership problem
→ Financial problems: Often a people + focus problem

Get the people right.
Build the systems.
Focus on the Hedgehog.
Everything else is noise.
