# 🚨 PROBLEM: I CAN'T GET CUSTOMERS
### The Complete Diagnostic & Treatment Plan
*When your pipeline is dry, your offer isn't landing, or growth has stalled*

---

## STEP 1: DIAGNOSE THE REAL PROBLEM

Before jumping to solutions, identify which problem you actually have:

```
DIAGNOSTIC TREE:

Do you have a product people want?
├── NO → You have a PRODUCT problem (start with Mom Test)
└── YES ↓

Do people know you exist?
├── NO → You have an AWARENESS problem
└── YES ↓

Do people understand what you do?
├── NO → You have a MESSAGING problem (StoryBrand)
└── YES ↓

Do people trust you enough to buy?
├── NO → You have a CREDIBILITY problem
└── YES ↓

Is your offer compelling enough?
├── NO → You have an OFFER problem ($100M Offers)
└── YES ↓

Is your price right?
├── NO → You have a PRICING problem
└── YES → You have a SALES PROCESS problem
```

## THE FRAMEWORKS THAT FIX THIS

### 🔧 FIX 1: THE MOM TEST (Wrong product)
**Source: The Mom Test — Rob Fitzpatrick**

Stop asking "Would you buy this?" Nobody will tell you the truth.
Ask about their LIFE, not your IDEA.

```
WRONG QUESTIONS:                RIGHT QUESTIONS:
"Do you like my idea?"          "What's the hardest part about X?"
"Would you pay for this?"       "Tell me about the last time this happened"
"What do you think?"            "What have you tried to solve this?"
                                "What would make you switch?"
                                "Who else should I talk to?"

THE 3 RULES:
1. Talk about their life, not your idea
2. Ask about specific past behavior, not hypotheticals
3. Talk less. Listen more.

GOOD CUSTOMER SIGNAL: They offer to pay before you ask
BAD SIGNAL: They say "interesting" or "I'd definitely use that"
```

```

What I think they need: [describe]

```

### 🔧 FIX 2: STORYBRAND (Wrong messaging)
**Source: Building a StoryBrand — Donald Miller**

If customers don't understand you in 5 seconds, they leave.

```
YOUR ONE-LINER FORMULA:
"We help [CUSTOMER] who struggle with [PROBLEM] 
 so they can [SUCCESS] without [FEAR]."

WEBSITE HEADLINE FORMULA:
Line 1: What you offer
Line 2: What their life looks like after
CTA: Clear button ("Get Started" / "Book a Call")

MOST COMMON MESSAGING MISTAKE:
Making the brand the hero instead of the customer.
"We are the leading provider of..." → Nobody cares.
"You deserve a home that feels like yours" → They feel it.
```

### 🔧 FIX 3: THE $100M OFFER (Wrong offer)
**Source: $100M Offers — Alex Hormozi**

A great product with a weak offer will lose to a mediocre product with a great offer.

```
OFFER AUDIT:
Score your offer 1-10 on each dimension:

Dream Outcome      ___/10  (how big is the prize?)
Perceived Likelihood ___/10 (how believable is it?)
Time to Result     ___/10  (how fast do they win?)
Effort Required    ___/10  (how easy is it for them?)

TOTAL OFFER SCORE: ___/40

Below 30: Your offer needs serious work
30-35: Good, needs refinement
35-40: Grand slam offer territory

TO IMPROVE YOUR OFFER:
→ Add guarantees that remove all risk
→ Add bonuses that solve the objections
→ Show a faster path to results
→ Make it more specific (not "results" — "$X in Y days")
```

### 🔧 FIX 5: CONTAGIOUS FRAMEWORK (No word of mouth)
**Source: Contagious — Jonah Berger**

Why do some things spread and others don't? STEPPS:

```
S — SOCIAL CURRENCY
    Does sharing it make people look good?
    → Make your customers feel like insiders

T — TRIGGERS
    What environmental cues remind people of you?
    → "Peanut butter" triggers jelly. What triggers you?

E — EMOTION
    High arousal emotions spread: awe, excitement, anger
    Low arousal don't: contentment, sadness
    → Make them feel something

P — PUBLIC
    Can people see others using it?
    → Build in social visibility

P — PRACTICAL VALUE
    Is it useful enough to share?
    → "News you can use"

S — STORIES
    What's the narrative that carries your message?
    → Stories are the vessels for information
```


## THE CUSTOMER ACQUISITION STACK
*Use these in sequence, not randomly*

```
STAGE 1: VALIDATE (0 customers)
Books: The Mom Test, Zero to One
Goal: Confirm people will pay before you build

STAGE 2: FIND YOUR FIRST 10 (1-10 customers)
Books: Never Eat Alone, The Challenger Sale
Goal: Manual, unscalable hustle to prove it works

STAGE 3: BUILD REPEATABLE PROCESS (10-100 customers)
Books: $100M Leads, Dotcom Secrets
Goal: Find one channel that works consistently

STAGE 4: SCALE WHAT WORKS (100-1000 customers)
Books: Contagious, Blitzscaling, Crossing the Chasm
Goal: Pour fuel on the fire that's already burning

STAGE 5: DEFEND AND EXPAND (1000+ customers)
Books: Blue Ocean Strategy, Good to Great
Goal: Build moats. Expand to adjacent markets.
```
