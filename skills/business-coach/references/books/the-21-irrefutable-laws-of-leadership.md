# BOOK 77: THE 21 IRREFUTABLE LAWS OF LEADERSHIP (Maxwell)

```
THE 5 MOST BUSINESS-CRITICAL LAWS:

1. THE LAW OF THE LID
Leadership ability is the lid on personal and organizational effectiveness.
If your leadership is a 6/10: Your organization maxes at 6/10.
Improve leadership first. Everything else follows.

2. THE LAW OF INFLUENCE
True leadership is influence — nothing more, nothing less.
Title doesn't give you leadership. Trust and respect do.
You can have a leadership title without influence.
You can have enormous influence without a title.

3. THE LAW OF PROCESS
Leadership develops daily, not in a day.
You can't shortcut it. You can only invest in it consistently.
Monthly leadership book, mentors, practice.

4. THE LAW OF ADDITION
Leaders add value by serving others.
The best leaders ask: "How do I add to the people around me?"
Not: "How do the people around me add to me?"

5. THE LAW OF REPRODUCTION
You can only raise up leaders to the level of your own leadership.
If you want better leaders in your organization: become a better leader.
You reproduce what you are.
```
