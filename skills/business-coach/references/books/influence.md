## THE 7 PRINCIPLES — BUSINESS APPLICATIONS

**RECIPROCITY IN BUSINESS:**
The free content model works because of reciprocity.
Give genuinely valuable content → Build obligation → Convert to clients.
The key: Give FIRST. Give MORE than expected. Give PERSONALLY.

Free checklist → good.
Free personalized audit → great.
Free strategy call where you give real advice → outstanding.

**COMMITMENT/CONSISTENCY IN SALES:**
The foot-in-the-door technique:
Small ask → Commitment → Larger ask aligned with the commitment.

"Would you agree that [problem they have] is holding your business back?"
YES → They've committed to having the problem.
Next: "We specialize in solving exactly that problem."
Their consistency drive makes them receptive.

**SOCIAL PROOF HIERARCHY:**
Most powerful: Case studies from people exactly like them.
"This worked for Sarah, a 3-person cleaning company in Phoenix..."

Video testimonials > Written testimonials
Specific results > General endorsements
Same industry + same size > Similar only in industry

**AUTHORITY SIGNALS THAT WORK:**
Published articles > Claimed expertise
Client logos > Self-descriptions
Awards > Self-proclamations
Specific credentials > Vague experience claims

Before the sales call: Authority should already be established.
"You should know before we talk that..." (email with authority signals)

**LIKING ACCELERATORS:**
Genuine interest in THEM (not your pitch) before anything else.
Find real common ground (not manufactured).
Compliment something specific and earned.
Mirror their communication style (pace, tone, vocabulary).

**SCARCITY SYSTEMS:**
Cohort enrollment (limited spots per quarter)
Application process (not everyone gets in)
Waitlist (signals demand)
MUST BE REAL. Fake scarcity kills trust.

**UNITY (newest principle):**
Build community identity around your brand.
"We" language: "People like us do things like this."
Create insider identity: "Claude moms" "The 5am club" "Bootstrappers"
