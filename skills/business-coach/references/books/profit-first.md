## THE ONE LINE
Flip the financial formula: Pay profit first, then run on what's left.

## THE SYSTEM — COMPLETE IMPLEMENTATION

```
STEP 1: OPEN 5 BANK ACCOUNTS
1. Income Account (all money comes in here)
2. Profit Account (your real profit — don't touch)
3. Owner's Compensation Account (your salary)
4. Tax Account (money you owe government)
5. Operating Expenses Account (everything else)

STEP 2: SET YOUR TARGET ALLOCATION PERCENTAGES (TAPs)

Starting percentages (adjust over time):
REVENUE RANGE    PROFIT  OWNER'S  TAX   OPEX
$0-$250K          5%      50%    15%   30%
$250K-$500K       10%     35%    15%   40%
$500K-$1M         15%     20%    15%   50%
$1M-$5M           15%     10%    15%   60%
$5M-$10M+         20%     5%     15%   60%

STEP 3: ALLOCATE EVERY 10TH AND 25TH OF THE MONTH
All money that came into Income Account:
→ Transfer exact % to each account
→ Pay bills only from Operating Expenses Account
→ Never touch Profit or Tax accounts (they're not yours)

STEP 4: TAKE YOUR QUARTERLY PROFIT DISTRIBUTION
On the last day of each quarter:
→ Take 50% of Profit Account as your bonus
→ Leave 50% as your buffer (for lean months)

STEP 5: ELIMINATE UNNECESSARY EXPENSES EACH QUARTER
Look at your OpEx Account: What didn't you actually need?
Cancel subscriptions, renegotiate contracts, eliminate waste.
The constraint forces creativity.
```

## THE REAL TAKEAWAY

The formula isn't about the percentages.
It's about SEEING profit as a non-negotiable expense rather than a remainder.

Most businesses: Revenue - Expenses = Profit (hope there's some left)
Profit First: Revenue - Profit = Expenses (constraint forces efficiency)

The psychology of the separate accounts:
→ If it's in the account, your brain sees it as available.
→ Keeping profit physically separate prevents spending it.
→ Same principle as 401k auto-contribution.
→ You don't spend what you can't see.
