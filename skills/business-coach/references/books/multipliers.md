## ARE YOU AN ACCIDENTAL DIMINISHER?

Most diminishers think they're helping.
They're actually shutting down the team around them.

```
COMMON ACCIDENTAL DIMINISHER BEHAVIORS:

THE IDEA FOUNTAIN:
You always have an idea. People stop sharing theirs.
Fix: Count your ideas in meetings. Share fewer. Ask more.

THE OPTIMIST:
You're always positive. People stop sharing problems.
Fix: Acknowledge challenges before celebrating.
"Yes this is hard. And I think we can figure it out."

THE RESCUER:
You solve problems for people. They stop solving their own.
Fix: "What have you tried?" before offering solutions.

THE PACESETTER:
You model excellence at high speed. Others can't keep up. They stop trying.
Fix: Set the vision, not the pace. Let the team find their rhythm.

THE RAPID RESPONDER:
You answer emails/messages instantly. Others feel pressure to do the same.
Fix: Batch your responses. Signal it's okay to have focused time.

THE ALWAYS ON:
You're always available. Others feel they can't make decisions without you.
Fix: Create space. Be unavailable sometimes. Force decisions to be made.
```

## THE MULTIPLIER EXPERIMENT

Take one diminishing behavior.
Commit to changing it for 2 weeks.

Track what happens:
→ Do people start contributing more ideas?
→ Do they solve problems before bringing them to you?
→ Do meetings become more energetic?
→ Do people seem more confident?

This experiment will surprise you. Every time.
