# BOOK 88: DOTCOM SECRETS (Russell Brunson)

```
THE VALUE LADDER:
Every business should have multiple price points.
Customers enter at the bottom and ascend.

FREE → $7-27 → $97-297 → $997-2997 → $10K+

The higher up the ladder: More value, more personal, higher price.

THE FUNNEL STRUCTURE:
Traffic → Lead Magnet → Email Sequence → Core Offer → Upsell → High Ticket

LEAD MAGNET: Solve one specific problem for free.
Must be so good they wonder what the paid version is like.

EMAIL SEQUENCE: Relationship builder before the sale.
5-7 emails: Story → Problem → Solution → Social proof → Offer

THE VALUE EQUATION FOR FUNNELS:
Frontend: Sell a lower-ticket item at near-zero margin.
Backend: The profit comes from repeat customers buying higher-ticket offers.
"Your first product should break even. Your backend should be your profit."

THE SECRET: Most businesses leave 90% of their revenue on the table
by not building the ladder. Customers who buy once will buy again
if asked at the right time for the right upgrade.
```
