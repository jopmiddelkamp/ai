# BOOK 93: WHEN BREATH BECOMES AIR (Kalanithi)

```
THE LESSON FOR ENTREPRENEURS:
Written by a neurosurgeon facing terminal cancer.
"What makes life meaningful — sufficient to make a life worth living?"

THE BUSINESS IMPLICATION:
Every entrepreneur eventually faces this question.
Usually when they've "made it" and feel empty.
Or when they've lost something important to build the business.

THE KALANITHI FRAMEWORK:
Meaning comes not from the destination but from the decision
to commit fully to something worth doing.
"The most important task is not to live, but to live well."

FOR YOUR BUSINESS:
Is this work worth doing?
Not financially — existentially.
If you couldn't talk about the revenue ever again:
Would you still be proud of what you're building?
If yes: You have meaning. If no: You have a job.
```
