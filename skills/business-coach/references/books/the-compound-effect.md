## THE ONE LINE
Small, consistent actions compound into extraordinary results — but the results are invisible until suddenly they're undeniable.

## THE COMPOUNDING FORMULA

```
Small Action × Consistency × Time = Extraordinary Result

THE INVISIBLE PHASE:
Months 1-6: No visible result (this is where most people quit)
Months 7-12: Small visible results (people stay if they lasted)
Year 2-3: Noticeable momentum
Year 5+: Results that seem impossible to others

THE PENNY METAPHOR:
$1M now OR one penny doubled every day for 31 days?
Day 1: $0.01
Day 10: $5.12
Day 20: $5,242.88
Day 31: $10,737,418.24

The compound effect is indistinguishable from nothing for most of its life.
Then it becomes everything.
```

## THE 3 STRATEGIES

```
1. CHOICES
Every choice has compound consequence.
"It's just one piece of cake" = repeated 365x = health crisis
"It's just one daily workout" = repeated 365x = physical transformation

THE CHOICE AUDIT:
For every recurring choice: What's the 10-year compound result?
"If I do exactly this every day for 10 years, where do I end up?"

2. HABITS
The daily routines that make choices automatic.
Build habits that compound in the right direction.
Break habits that compound in the wrong direction.

THE HABIT TRACKING SYSTEM:
Track one habit per sheet. Mark every day.
See the streak. Protect the streak.
Visual compounding is motivating.

3. MOMENTUM
Once momentum exists: the compound effect accelerates.
Maintain momentum through the invisible phase.
The hardest time is always before the breakthrough.

THE MOMENTUM STARTER:
Begin smaller than feels significant.
"I'll work out for 5 minutes" is a beginning.
You'll almost always do more once you start.
Starting is the hardest part. Make starting trivial.
```

## THE INPUT/OUTPUT MODEL

```
Your life is the output of your inputs.
Control the inputs → Control the outputs.

INPUTS:
→ Information: What you read, watch, listen to
→ Associations: Who you spend time with
→ Environment: Physical spaces you inhabit

Most people change their goals (outputs) without changing their inputs.
Nothing changes.

THE ASSOCIATION AUDIT:
List your 5 closest relationships.
Are they raising your average or lowering it?
"You are the average of the 5 people you spend the most time with." — Rohn

This is harsh and true.
```
