# BOOK 39: BLITZSCALING

## THE ONE LINE
Blitzscaling means prioritizing speed over efficiency in the face of uncertainty — acceptable when you're racing to capture a market.

## WHEN TO BLITZSCALE

```
BLITZSCALE WHEN:
→ Winner takes all (or most) market dynamics exist
→ Capital is available
→ You have product-market fit (not before!)
→ Delay means losing to a competitor who will blitzscale

DO NOT BLITZSCALE WHEN:
→ Market doesn't have network effects
→ You don't have product-market fit
→ Capital isn't available to sustain rapid growth
→ Quality of growth matters more than speed

THE EXAMPLES:
LinkedIn, Airbnb, Uber — all blitzscaled.
Most businesses should NOT blitzscale.
Blitzscaling has enormous costs:
→ Culture breaks
→ Management drowns
→ Quality suffers
→ Cash burns
The upside: You win the market before anyone else can.
```

## THE 5 STAGES OF BLITZSCALING

```
Stage 1: FAMILY (1-9 employees)
Everyone wears every hat.
No processes. Pure chaos and creativity.
Success factor: Product-market fit.

Stage 2: TRIBE (10-99 employees)
Founder knows everyone.
Informal culture. Processes start forming.
Success factor: Hire managers before you need them.

Stage 3: VILLAGE (100-999 employees)
Founder knows most people.
Management layer needed.
Functions emerge: Sales, Marketing, Engineering, HR.
Success factor: Professional management. Culture documentation.

Stage 4: CITY (1,000-9,999 employees)
Founder knows only leaders.
Formal processes mandatory.
Risk of bureaucracy vs. innovation.
Success factor: Maintain innovation while adding structure.

Stage 5: NATION (10,000+ employees)
Founder is more CEO than builder.
Every function is professionalized.
Success factor: Decentralization. Autonomous divisions.
```

## THE COUNTERINTUITIVE RULES OF BLITZSCALING

```
1. EMBRACE CHAOS
Optimization slows you down when speed is the goal.
Accept inefficiency as the price of speed.
Fix it later (if there is a later).

2. HIRE MS. RIGHTSNOW, NOT MS. RIGHT
The person who fits your company today may not fit it in 2 years.
Hire for the current stage. Build in planned transitions.

3. TOLERATE "GOOD ENOUGH"
Perfection at 10x your current scale will look wrong anyway.
Ship it. Learn. Iterate.

4. IGNORE YOUR CUSTOMERS (sometimes)
Customer feedback optimizes for current scale.
Blitzscaling requires building for next scale.
"If I had asked what they wanted, they would have said a faster horse."

5. RAISE MORE MONEY THAN YOU THINK YOU NEED
Blitzscaling burns cash faster than projections.
Always. Every time.
The successful ones raised too much, not too little.
```
