## THE ONE LINE
Most wealthy people don't look wealthy — they built wealth by living below their means consistently for decades.

## THE 7 COMMON FACTORS OF WEALTHY PEOPLE

```
1. THEY LIVE WELL BELOW THEIR MEANS
Not frugal — intentional.
Drive ordinary cars. Live in moderate neighborhoods.
Their neighbors don't know they're wealthy.

2. THEY ALLOCATE TIME, ENERGY, AND MONEY EFFICIENTLY
They think about wealth building consistently.
They're intentional about their financial decisions.

3. THEY BELIEVE FINANCIAL INDEPENDENCE IS MORE IMPORTANT THAN SOCIAL STATUS
They don't buy things to impress others.
They buy freedom.

4. THEIR PARENTS DID NOT SUBSIDIZE THEM
Adult financial support (Economic Outpatient Care) undermines wealth building.
Receiving money teaches you to receive money, not create it.

5. THEIR ADULT CHILDREN ARE ECONOMICALLY SELF-SUFFICIENT
They teach their children to be independent.
They don't give them money — they give them skills.

6. THEY ARE PROFICIENT AT TARGETING MARKET OPPORTUNITIES
Most are business owners or self-employed.
They built wealth through building companies, not climbing ladders.

7. THEY CHOSE THE RIGHT OCCUPATION
Not necessarily the highest-paying.
The occupation that rewards independence, ownership, and variable pay.
Business ownership > salary in wealth building.

THE WEALTH FORMULA:
Expected Net Worth = (Age × Pretax Annual Income) / 10

If your actual net worth is above this: You're a Prodigious Accumulator of Wealth (PAW)
If below: You're an Under Accumulator of Wealth (UAW)
```
