## THE ONE LINE
The 6-week personal finance program that automates your finances so you can spend guilt-free on what you love.

## THE AUTOMATION SYSTEM

```
PAYCHECK ARRIVES
    ↓
AUTOMATED TRANSFERS (on the same day):
→ 401k contribution (to employer match at minimum)
→ Roth IRA contribution (if eligible)
→ Fixed savings (emergency fund, goals)
→ Fixed investments (index funds)
→ Fixed bills (rent, utilities, car payment)
    ↓
CHECKING ACCOUNT REMAINDER
→ Variable spending (food, clothes, entertainment)
→ THIS IS YOUR GUILT-FREE SPENDING ZONE

THE RULE: Once the automation runs, you can spend everything left in checking.
No guilt. No tracking. The important stuff already happened automatically.
```

## THE CONSCIOUS SPENDING PLAN

```
Not a budget (restrictive, focused on deprivation).
A spending plan (intentional, focused on what you value).

4 CATEGORIES:
Fixed costs (50-60%): Rent, utilities, insurance, car payment
Investments (10%): Retirement, index funds
Savings (5-10%): Emergency fund, vacation, goals
Guilt-free spending (20-35%): Whatever you want

THE KEY INSIGHT:
You don't need to optimize every dollar.
You need to optimize the BIG decisions (housing, car, career)
and automate the system.
Then spend freely within the plan.

THE CONSCIOUS SPENDING PRINCIPLE:
Identify the 3-5 things you value most in life.
Spend lavishly on those.
Cut ruthlessly on everything else.
(Sethi: Nice apartment + cheap car + travel + not dining out much)
```
