# BOOK 92: BECOMING (Michelle Obama)

```
THE LEADERSHIP LESSONS FROM THE MEMOIR:

1. YOUR STORY IS YOUR POWER
Obama never hid her South Side Chicago background.
She used it. It was her authenticity, her credibility, her connection.

FOR ENTREPRENEURS:
Your background, your struggles, your unconventional path —
these are not things to hide.
They're what makes you trustworthy to the people you serve.

2. EXCELLENCE IS NON-NEGOTIABLE
"Am I good enough?" Yes, you are. Now let's get to work.
Not "am I good enough" as a question — as a starting premise.

3. LIFT AS YOU CLIMB
As you rise, bring others with you.
The measure of your success isn't just what you built —
it's who you helped along the way.

FOR BUSINESS:
Building a business that creates opportunities for others
is both the ethical and the business-smart move.
People who feel invested in your mission work harder for it.
```
