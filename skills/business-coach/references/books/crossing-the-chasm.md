## THE ONE LINE
Between early adopters and mainstream market lies "the chasm" — and most businesses fall into it.

## THE TECHNOLOGY ADOPTION LIFECYCLE

```
     INNOVATORS  EARLY      EARLY     LATE      LAGGARDS
     (2.5%)    ADOPTERS    MAJORITY  MAJORITY   (16%)
               (13.5%)     (34%)     (34%)

        ↑          ↑    ↑↑   ↑         ↑
     Visionaries  Chasm  Pragmatists  Conservatives

THE CHASM: The gap between Early Adopters and Early Majority

Early Adopters: Love new technology. Accept risk. Want revolutionary change.
Early Majority: Skeptical. Want proven solutions. Want evolutionary improvement.

THESE ARE COMPLETELY DIFFERENT CUSTOMERS:
Early Adopter thinks: "If it doesn't work, no problem — I'll find a workaround."
Early Majority thinks: "If it doesn't work perfectly, I'm switching back."

The strategy that wins Early Adopters FAILS with Early Majority.
```

## THE D-DAY STRATEGY

To cross the chasm: Dominate a niche segment completely.
Then use that as a beachhead to expand.

```
STEP 1: IDENTIFY THE BOWLING PIN
Which specific niche, if you dominated it completely,
would give you the beachhead you need?

Criteria:
→ Big enough to be worth winning
→ Small enough to win completely
→ Has strong word-of-mouth connections to adjacent segments

STEP 2: WIN THAT NICHE COMPLETELY
100% of that niche should know you.
Create a complete solution: product + ecosystem + support + community

STEP 3: LET ADJACENT NICHES HEAR ABOUT IT
When the "bowling pin" tips: other pins fall.
Word travels from niche to niche.
Your reference customers give credibility to new segments.

EXAMPLE:
Salesforce: Started with small startups (couldn't afford Siebel)
            Dominated that segment
            Used references to move upmarket
            Now serves Enterprise
```

## THE WHOLE PRODUCT CONCEPT

Mainstream buyers don't buy products. They buy SOLUTIONS.

The whole product = product + everything needed to succeed with it.

```
CORE PRODUCT: What ships
EXPECTED PRODUCT: Minimum to deliver the value promise
AUGMENTED PRODUCT: Exceeds expectations
POTENTIAL PRODUCT: All possible improvements

FOR YOUR BUSINESS:
What does the customer need BEYOND your core product to achieve the result?
→ Training
→ Implementation support
→ Templates and tools
→ Community
→ Integrations
→ Customer success
→ Support

Most businesses sell the core product.
Chasm-crossers sell the whole product.
```
