# BOOK 78: LEADERS EAT LAST (Sinek)

```
THE ONE LINE: The best leaders sacrifice their own comfort for those in their care.

THE CIRCLE OF SAFETY:
Great leaders create an environment where people feel safe inside the organization,
allowing them to face threats from outside together.

When the circle of safety is strong:
→ People don't spend energy on internal politics
→ Trust is high → collaboration is high
→ Everyone faces external challenges together

When the circle of safety is weak:
→ Energy goes to internal politics
→ People protect themselves from each other
→ External threats go unanswered because everyone is fighting internally

THE CHEMICALS OF LEADERSHIP:
ENDORPHINS: Mask physical pain (we feel good doing hard work together)
DOPAMINE: Reward achievement (celebrate milestones)
SEROTONIN: Pride from being valued by the group (recognition)
OXYTOCIN: Safety of deep human bonds (genuine care)
CORTISOL: The stress chemical (produced by bad leadership)

Best leaders: Create environments high in serotonin and oxytocin.
Worst leaders: Create environments high in cortisol (fear).
Cortisol-rich environments: High turnover, low creativity, poor health outcomes.
```
