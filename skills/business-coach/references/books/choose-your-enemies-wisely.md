# BOOK 96: CHOOSE YOUR ENEMIES WISELY (Bet-David)

```
THE ONE LINE: Your enemies define you as much as your friends — choose them deliberately.

THE FRAMEWORK:
Enemies (opposition, competitors, naysayers) create:
→ Clarity on your differentiation
→ Fuel for sustained motivation
→ A story for your audience to rally around

THE ENEMY LIST:
Not individual people — beliefs, systems, broken industries.
"We're against the idea that you have to sacrifice your family for a successful career."
"We're against the belief that you need VC money to build a great business."
"We're against the assumption that AI will replace humans."

ENEMIES FOR BRAND BUILDING:
The brands with the clearest enemies have the clearest positioning.
Apple's enemy: Conformity and complexity (Windows)
Nike's enemy: Limits and "I can't"
Patagonia's enemy: Environmental destruction

YOUR ENEMY: ________________________
Your brand becomes clearest when you define what you're against.
```
