# BOOK 91: THE ALCHEMIST (Coelho)

```
THE ONE LINE: When you want something with all your heart, the universe conspires to help you achieve it.

THE BUSINESS LESSON (often missed):
The Alchemist is not a business book.
But it contains the most important business principle:

"There is one great truth on this planet: whoever you are, 
or whatever it is that you do, when you really want something, 
it's because that desire originated in the soul of the universe."

THE ENTREPRENEURIAL APPLICATION:
Your most persistent desire — the one that keeps returning
despite all evidence it's impractical — is probably your Personal Legend.

Most entrepreneurs suppress their Personal Legend for a "realistic" version.
The entrepreneurs who build extraordinary things are usually
pursuing their Personal Legend, not a market opportunity.

"Tell your heart that the fear of suffering is worse 
than the suffering itself."
```
