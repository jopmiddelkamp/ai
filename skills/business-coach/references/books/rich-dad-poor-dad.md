## THE ONE LINE
The rich don't work for money — they make money work for them through assets.

## THE ASSET vs. LIABILITY FRAMEWORK

```
WHAT MOST PEOPLE THINK:
Asset = Things I own (house, car, boat)
Liability = Things I owe money on

KIYOSAKI'S DEFINITION:
Asset = Puts money IN your pocket
Liability = Takes money OUT of your pocket

RECALCULATED:
Your home (if mortgaged): USUALLY a LIABILITY (mortgage payment out)
Your car: ALWAYS a LIABILITY (payments, insurance, depreciation out)
Rental property: ASSET (rent in, net positive)
Business that runs without you: ASSET (cash flow in)
Stocks: ASSET (dividends, appreciation)
Gold/commodities: MAYBE (depends on market)
```

## THE FINANCIAL EDUCATION GAP

Kiyosaki's core argument: Schools teach how to work for money.
They don't teach how money works.

THE 5 FINANCIAL CONCEPTS NOT TAUGHT IN SCHOOL:
1. How taxes work (and how to legally minimize them)
2. How corporations protect wealth
3. How debt can be an asset (good debt vs. bad debt)
4. How to read financial statements
5. How investments compound
