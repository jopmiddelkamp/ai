## THE ONE LINE
A step-by-step guide from $0 to financial freedom through aggressive savings, house hacking, and smart career moves.

## THE 3 STAGES TO FINANCIAL FREEDOM

```
STAGE 1: $0 to $25K (Financial Stability)
Focus: Stop the bleeding. Build the base.
→ Reduce housing cost (house hack: rent rooms, live for free)
→ Eliminate all consumer debt
→ Build 6-month emergency fund
→ Maximize income (side income, career advancement)

STAGE 2: $25K to $100K (Financial Security)
Focus: Invest aggressively while lifestyle remains modest.
→ Invest in income-producing assets (real estate, index funds)
→ Avoid lifestyle inflation aggressively
→ Continue house hacking or rent minimization
→ Develop high-income skills

STAGE 3: $100K+ (Financial Freedom)
Focus: Put money to work, not yourself.
→ Asset income > expense income
→ Can choose what work to do based on meaning, not money
→ Pursue highest leverage activities only

HOUSE HACKING (Trench's primary strategy):
Buy a small multi-unit property (duplex, triplex).
Live in one unit. Rent others.
Your tenants pay your mortgage. You live for free.
Build equity. Repeat.

For entrepreneurs:
The money not spent on rent = investment capital.
Even $2,000/month savings = $24K/year = another business acquisition in 5 years.
```
