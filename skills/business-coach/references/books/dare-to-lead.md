# BOOK 76: DARE TO LEAD (Brené Brown)

```
THE ONE LINE: Vulnerability is not weakness — it's the courage that makes real leadership possible.

THE 4 SKILL SETS OF DARING LEADERSHIP:

1. RUMBLING WITH VULNERABILITY
Showing up when you can't control the outcome.
"I don't know." "I made a mistake." "I need help."
These phrases create the psychological safety that makes teams great.

2. LIVING INTO YOUR VALUES
Identifying your 2 core values.
Making decisions that honor them even when difficult.
The leader who lives their values creates permission for the team to do the same.

3. BRAVING TRUST (BRAVING ACRONYM)
B = Boundaries (you honor mine and I honor yours)
R = Reliability (you do what you say)
A = Accountability (you own mistakes)
V = Vault (you don't share information inappropriately)
I = Integrity (you choose courage over comfort)
N = Non-judgment (I can ask without judgment)
G = Generosity (you extend the most generous interpretation)

4. LEARNING TO RISE
Failing well. Recovering from setbacks.
The reckoning: Acknowledge the emotion (don't stuff it)
The rumble: Get curious about the story (what's actually happening)
The revolution: Change the story based on reality (not the fear narrative)

THE ARMORED LEADERSHIP vs. DARING LEADERSHIP:
Armored: Drive perfectionism, controlling, uses shame for accountability
Daring: Model healthy striving, gives clear expectations, uses accountability without shame
```
