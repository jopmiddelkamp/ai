## THE ONE LINE
A complete operating system for any business under 250 people — six tools that eliminate chaos and create traction.

## WHY THIS BOOK EXISTS
Most entrepreneurs are great at their craft but terrible at running a business. They have vision but no system. Energy but no direction. Great people but no accountability. Traction gives you the rails to run on.

## THE CORE FRAMEWORK: EOS (Entrepreneurial Operating System)

```
THE 6 COMPONENTS — Every business needs all 6 to thrive:

①  VISION ──────────────────────────────────────────────────
   Everyone in the organization knows where you're going
   and how you're going to get there.
   
   Tools: V/TO (Vision/Traction Organizer)
   
   The 8 Questions:
   1. What are your core values?
   2. What is your core focus? (purpose + niche)
   3. What is your 10-year target?
   4. What is your marketing strategy?
   5. What is your 3-year picture?
   6. What is your 1-year plan?
   7. What are your quarterly rocks?
   8. What are your issues?

②  PEOPLE ───────────────────────────────────────────────────
   Right people in right seats.
   
   Right Person = Shares your core values
   Right Seat   = GWC the role:
                  G = Gets it
                  W = Wants it  
                  C = Has Capacity
   
   Tool: People Analyzer
   Rate every person on every core value: + / +/- / -
   Anyone with two or more minuses = wrong person

③  DATA ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   Cut through subjectivity. Run your business on numbers.
   
   Tool: The Scorecard
   5-15 numbers that give you a weekly pulse on the business
   Each number has an owner and a weekly goal
   Green = on track, Red = off track
   
   Examples:
   • Revenue vs. target
   • Proposals sent
   • Calls made
   • Customer satisfaction score
   • Cash on hand
   • Accounts receivable over 60 days

④  ISSUES ───────────────────────────────────────────────────
   Identify, discuss, and solve problems at the root cause.
   
   Tool: IDS (Identify, Discuss, Solve)
   
   Identify: What is the real issue? (usually not the stated issue)
   Discuss: Dig in. Get all perspectives. Be honest.
   Solve: Make a decision. Create a to-do. Move on.
   
   Issues List = Your running list of problems to solve
   Long-Term Issues: Deal with in quarterly sessions
   Short-Term Issues: Deal with in weekly meetings

⑤  PROCESS ──────────────────────────────────────────────────
   Systemize your business so it runs without you.
   
   Tool: The 3-Step Process Documenter
   Step 1: Identify your 6-10 core processes
   Step 2: Document them (high-level, not 50-page manuals)
   Step 3: Follow them by everyone, every time
   
   Core processes in most businesses:
   • HR process (hire to fire)
   • Marketing process (generate leads)
   • Sales process (lead to close)
   • Operations process (deliver the thing)
   • Customer retention process
   • Finance process (P&L to tax)

⑥  TRACTION ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
   Execute on your vision with discipline and accountability.
   
   Tools: Rocks + Meeting Pulse
   
   ROCKS: 90-day priorities
   • 3-7 per person, per quarter
   • Specific, measurable, achievable in 90 days
   • Everything else is sand
   
   MEETING PULSE:
   • Annual: 2-day planning session
   • Quarterly: 1-day planning session  
   • Weekly: 90-minute L10 meeting
   • Daily: Huddle (5-10 min, stand-up)
```

## THE L10 MEETING (Weekly 90 Minutes)

```
L10 = Level 10 Meeting. Rated 10/10 every week when done right.

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ SEGUE (5 min)                                       │
│ Each person shares: one personal + one professional  │
│ good news from the past week                        │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ SCORECARD REVIEW (5 min)                            │
│ Review each number. Drop underperforming ones to    │
│ Issues list. No debate here.                        │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ ROCK REVIEW (5 min)                                 │
│ Each rock: On track or Off track? No discussion.    │
│ Off track → Issues list.                            │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ CUSTOMER/EMPLOYEE HEADLINES (5 min)                 │
│ Any good or bad news worth sharing? Issues list if  │
│ needs solving.                                      │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ TO-DO LIST (5 min)                                  │
│ Review last week's to-dos. Done or not done.        │
│ Not done → Why? Move or drop.                       │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ IDS — ISSUES SOLVING (60 min) ← THE MONEY          │
│ Work through issues list by priority.               │
│ For each: Identify the real problem (not symptom)   │
│ Discuss: 1 minute max before solving                │
│ Solve: To-do with owner and deadline                │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ CONCLUDE (5 min)                                    │
│ Review to-dos created                               │
│ Cascading messages (what gets shared with team)     │
│ Rate the meeting 1-10                               │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## THE ACCOUNTABILITY CHART (Not Org Chart)

```
Most org charts show hierarchy. Accountability charts show function.

STRUCTURE:
┌──────────────────────────────────┐
│         VISIONARY                │
│  (Right brain: vision, culture,  │
│   relationships, big ideas)      │
└──────────────┬───────────────────┘
               │
┌──────────────▼───────────────────┐
│         INTEGRATOR               │
│  (Left brain: executes vision,   │
│   manages P&L, team, process)    │
└──────────────┬───────────────────┘
               │
    ┌──────────┼──────────┐
    ▼          ▼          ▼
  SALES/     OPERATIONS  FINANCE
  MARKETING

Every seat has:
• 5 Roles (things they're responsible for)
• GWC check (does this person GWC this seat?)
```

## KEY TAKEAWAYS

1. **The Visionary/Integrator pair is everything.** Most founders are Visionaries. They need an Integrator to execute. Finding yours is the most important hire you'll make.

2. **Rocks create focus.** 90 days is long enough to accomplish something meaningful, short enough to stay urgent. Everything not on your rock list is noise.

3. **Issues lists are gold.** When you have a safe place to put problems without needing to solve them immediately, you reduce anxiety and create better meetings.

4. **The L10 meeting is a discipline.** Most teams rate their meetings 4-6/10. EOS teams consistently rate 8-10. The structure is what makes the difference.

5. **Core values must be real.** Don't aspirationally list values you want to have. List the values you actually hire and fire by. If you wouldn't fire someone for violating it, it's not a core value.

## APPLIED TO YOUR BUSINESS

**Questions to answer today:**
- What are our 3-5 core values? (Would we fire someone for violating these?)
- Who is our Integrator? (Or do we need to hire one?)
- What are this quarter's 3-5 Rocks for the leadership team?
- What are our 5-10 weekly scorecard numbers?
- Are we having a real L10 meeting or just a status update?
