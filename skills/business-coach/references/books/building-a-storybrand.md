# BOOK 98: BUILDING A STORYBRAND — FINAL APPLICATIONS

```
(Extended from earlier coverage)

THE WEBSITE WIREFRAME (based on SB7):

SECTION 1: HEADER
What you offer + What life looks like after + CTA button
(Above the fold — everything needed in first 5 seconds)

SECTION 2: THE PROBLEM
State the villain and the problems it creates (external + internal + philosophical)

SECTION 3: THE GUIDE
Brief authority statement + Empathy statement + Number of people helped

SECTION 4: THE PLAN
3-step simple plan. Make it easy to start.

SECTION 5: CALL TO ACTION
Direct CTA (prominent, repeated)
Transitional CTA (for people not ready yet)

SECTION 6: FAILURE
What happens if they don't act (stakes)

SECTION 7: SUCCESS
What life looks like after working with you

SECTION 8: SOCIAL PROOF
Testimonials, logos, case studies, metrics

SECTION 9: FINAL CTA
Repeat the direct CTA
```
