## THE ONE LINE
You're over-saving and under-living. The goal isn't maximum wealth at death — it's maximum life experience before death.

## THE 9 RULES FOR MAXIMIZING YOUR LIFE

```
1. MAXIMIZE YOUR POSITIVE EXPERIENCES
Life experiences are the true measure of a life well-lived.
Not net worth.

2. START INVESTING IN EXPERIENCES EARLY
Health + Time + Money must all align for optimal experiences.
Young: Health ✓ Time ✓ Money ✗ — Borrow if needed
Middle: Health ✓ Time ✗ Money ✓ — Make time
Old: Health ✗ Time ✓ Money ✓ — Plan earlier experiences

3. AIM TO DIE WITH ZERO
Every dollar you have at death is a dollar you didn't experience.
Plan your drawdown, not just your accumulation.

4. USE ALL YOUR INCOME (after sensible investing)
Surviving on 10% of income while earning $500K = financial hoarding

5. GIVE MONEY TO YOUR KIDS WHEN IT MATTERS
$50K when they're 30 changes their life.
$500K when they're 60 is nice but not life-changing.

6. DON'T LIVE YOUR LIFE ON AUTOPILOT
Consciously choose your experiences.
The "someday" list almost never gets executed.
Put it on the calendar.

7. THINK OF LIFE AS SEASONS
Each season has different optimal experiences.
Some experiences are only possible when young and healthy.
Don't save them for "retirement."

8. KNOW WHEN ENOUGH IS ENOUGH
At some point, more money adds very little marginal happiness.
Know your "enough" number. Stop the accumulation machine.

9. TAKE BIG RISKS EARLIER IN LIFE
Asymmetric risk: Younger you has time to recover from failures.
Older you doesn't.
Most people get more conservative as they get richer.
Should be the opposite in some respects.
```

## THE MEMORY DIVIDEND

Experiences generate a "memory dividend" — you re-experience them in memory forever.

$5,000 spent on a family trip to Italy:
→ Experienced for 2 weeks
→ Remembered (and re-lived in memory) for 40+ years
→ Stories told to grandchildren
→ ROI: Infinite

$5,000 sitting in a money market account:
→ Experienced as: "I have this money"
→ Remembered as: "I have this money"
→ ROI: 4-5% per year

The money market account has better financial ROI.
The trip has infinitely better life ROI.
