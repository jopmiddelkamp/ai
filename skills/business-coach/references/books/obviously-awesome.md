## THE ONE LINE
Positioning determines how customers understand, value, and buy your product — most companies do it wrong.

## THE 5 COMPONENTS OF POSITIONING

```
1. COMPETITIVE ALTERNATIVES
What would your customer use if you didn't exist?
(Not just direct competitors — includes status quo, workarounds)

IMPORTANT: Don't define this based on who you compete with.
Define it based on what your best customers would do without you.

2. UNIQUE ATTRIBUTES
What do you have that alternatives don't?
Features, capabilities, history, team, integrations.
List everything. Then narrow to what's genuinely differentiated.

3. VALUE (what the attributes enable)
Attributes are features. Value is what those features do for the customer.
"We process 10x faster" = Attribute
"You get results in minutes instead of hours" = Value

The customer doesn't care about features.
They care about what those features do for their life/business.

4. TARGET CUSTOMER CHARACTERISTICS
Which customers value your differentiated value most?
Not "all small businesses" — which specific type?

The customer who finds your unique value most compelling
is your best target. Find more of them.

5. MARKET CATEGORY
What category are you in?
This frames everything: who compares you, how they evaluate you,
what they expect.

The WRONG category kills great products.
The RIGHT category makes good products obviously awesome.
```

## THE MARKET CATEGORY DECISION

```
3 OPTIONS:

1. EXISTING MARKET
Win: Immediate market understanding
Lose: Compared to established leaders on their terms
Best for: You're clearly better than alternatives on existing criteria

2. NEW MARKET (create a category)
Win: No competition, full control of definition
Lose: Expensive to educate, no existing demand
Best for: Truly revolutionary product, well-funded

3. RESEGMENTED MARKET (repositioning)
Win: Escape head-to-head competition
Lose: Must convince market of new segmentation
Best for: You're best for a SPECIFIC part of existing market

EXAMPLE:
Salesforce: Didn't compete with Siebel as "enterprise CRM"
            Repositioned as "no software CRM for small business"
            New category. Escaped competition.
```
