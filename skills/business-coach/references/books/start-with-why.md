## THE ONE LINE
People don't buy what you do. They buy WHY you do it.

## THE GOLDEN CIRCLE

```
       ┌─────────────────────────────────┐
       │            WHAT                 │  ← What you do (every company knows this)
       │    ┌───────────────────────┐    │
       │    │         HOW           │    │  ← How you do it (some companies know this)
       │    │    ┌──────────────┐   │    │
       │    │    │     WHY      │   │    │  ← Why you exist (most don't know)
       │    │    └──────────────┘   │    │
       │    └───────────────────────┘    │
       └─────────────────────────────────┘

Most companies communicate: WHAT → HOW → WHY
"We make great computers. They're beautiful and simple. Want to buy one?"

Apple communicates: WHY → HOW → WHAT
"Everything we do, we believe in challenging the status quo.
 We believe in thinking differently.
 The way we challenge the status quo is by making our products
 beautifully designed, simple to use, and user-friendly.
 And we happen to make great computers."

Same company. Same products. Completely different emotional resonance.
```

## WHY "WHY" WORKS (THE NEUROSCIENCE)

WHAT → Neocortex (rational brain, language, logic)
WHY → Limbic brain (emotions, behavior, decisions)

Decisions are made in the limbic brain.
Justified in the neocortex.

You think you choose with logic.
You actually choose with emotion.
Logic comes after to justify the choice you've already made.

This is why: "Our product has 47 features" → head nod.
And: "We believe you deserve tools that give you your time back" → gut feeling.

## FINDING YOUR WHY

It's not invented. It's discovered.
Usually rooted in your origin story.

Prompts:
→ Why did you start this? (real reason, not the polished one)
→ What problem made you angry enough to do something?
→ Who are you fighting for?
→ What does the world look like when you succeed?

YOUR WHY STRUCTURE:
"To __________ [contribution] so that __________ [impact]."

Example: "To show ambitious women that they can build extraordinary 
         lives without choosing between family and ambition."

## THE JUST CAUSE (Sinek's expansion in "The Infinite Game")

A Just Cause goes beyond your WHY.
It's a vision of the future that you can contribute to
but never fully achieve.

Properties of a Just Cause:
→ For something (not against something)
→ Open and inclusive (others can join)
→ Idealistic (always something more to do)
→ Durable (can outlast any individual)

EXAMPLES:
"A world where everyone has access to clean water"
"Proof that you can build a great business and be a great parent"
"AI that empowers, not replaces, human potential"

When you have a Just Cause: You can work harder for longer.
Because you're not working for the result. You're working for the cause.
