# BOOK 75: TURN THE SHIP AROUND (David Marquet)

```
THE ONE LINE: Move control to the information, not information to the control.

THE LEADER-LEADER vs. LEADER-FOLLOWER MODEL:

LEADER-FOLLOWER (traditional):
Leader thinks → Leader decides → Followers execute
Bottleneck: The leader. Everything waits for them.

LEADER-LEADER (Marquet's model):
Leaders at every level think and decide within their domain.
The top leader provides context and constraints.
Every person is responsible for their domain.

THE SANTA FE SUBMARINE:
Marquet took command of worst-performing sub in the US Navy.
In 12 months: Best-performing sub.

KEY BEHAVIORAL CHANGES:
Old: "Request permission to [action]"
New: "I intend to [action]" → [pause for objection only if needed]

Old: "What should I do about X?"
New: "I've identified X, here are 3 options, I recommend option 2, 
     unless you have concerns I'm not aware of."

THE RESULT:
Leadership permeates the organization.
Speed of decision-making increases dramatically.
Morale increases (people feel capable and trusted).
```
