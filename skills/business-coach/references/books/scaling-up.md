The Rockefeller Habits give you the operating system for $10M-$1B companies — more sophisticated than EOS for complex organizations.

## THE 4 DECISIONS EVERY COMPANY MUST GET RIGHT

```
1. PEOPLE
   Right people in the right roles with clear accountabilities
   A-player density at every level
   
2. STRATEGY
   Differentiated position that creates value
   Brand promise that's compelling and deliverable
   
3. EXECUTION
   Flawless execution of the strategy
   Rhythms and routines that create alignment
   
4. CASH
   Maintain adequate cash to fuel growth
   Cash flow is more important than profitability
   
THE RULE: A company can only grow as fast as its weakest of these 4.
```

## THE MEETING RHYTHM

```
DAILY HUDDLE (15 minutes, standing):
→ What's up (24-hour window)
→ Daily metrics
→ Where are you stuck?

WEEKLY MEETING (60-90 minutes):
→ Numbers update
→ Customer/employee feedback
→ Top 3 priorities
→ One key topic for in-depth discussion

MONTHLY MEETING (half day):
→ Department deep dives
→ Strategic issues
→ People matters

QUARTERLY PLANNING (2 days offsite):
→ Close out last quarter's rocks
→ Assess company health
→ Set next quarter's priorities
→ Address strategic issues

ANNUAL PLANNING (3 days):
→ Review/refresh BHAG and core purpose
→ Set annual plan
→ 3-year snapshot
→ Identify critical issues for coming year

WHY THIS MATTERS:
Most companies have too many meetings at the wrong times.
This rhythm eliminates unnecessary meetings while ensuring
nothing important falls through.
```

## THE 7 STRATA OF STRATEGY

```
1. WORDS THAT OWN A WORD (brand positioning)
   What word do you own in the mind of your customer?
   Volvo = Safety, FedEx = Overnight, Google = Search
   
2. SANDBOX AND BRAND PROMISES
   Where do you play? What do you promise?
   
3. BRAND PROMISE GUARANTEE
   What do you guarantee? If not delivered, what happens?
   
4. ONE-PHRASE STRATEGY (differentiator)
   What makes you genuinely different?
   Walmart: "Save money. Live better."
   Southwest: "The low-fare airline"
   
5. X FACTOR (10x-100x underlying advantage)
   What enables your differentiator structurally?
   
6. PROFIT PER X (economic engine)
   As in Hedgehog Concept — your key unit economics metric
   
7. BHAG (Big Hairy Audacious Goal)
   10-25 year target that excites and focuses the organization
```
