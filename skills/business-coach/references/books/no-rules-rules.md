## THE ONE LINE
The best talent strategy: pay top of market, expect excellence, remove all mediocrity immediately.

## THE NETFLIX TALENT PHILOSOPHY

```
CONVENTIONAL WISDOM:
Good enough employees + Clear rules = Acceptable company
NETFLIX LOGIC:
Exceptional employees + No rules = Exceptional company

THE MATH:
10 mediocre employees = 10 units of output
2 exceptional employees = 8 units of output
(but with fraction of management cost, much higher quality)

THE TALENT DENSITY PRINCIPLE:
The best asset in any company is dense talent.
Every mediocre employee reduces the performance of everyone around them.
The best people leave when surrounded by mediocrity.
Conclusion: It is ALWAYS worth paying to remove mediocrity.
```

## THE KEEPER TEST

"If [employee] told me tomorrow they were leaving for a competitor,
how hard would I fight to keep them?"

If the answer is "I'd fight hard" → They're a keeper.
If the answer is "I'd be disappointed but it would be okay" → Give them severance now.
If the answer is "I'd be relieved" → Why haven't you acted yet?

APPLY THIS QUARTERLY.
Not to be cruel — to be honest.
Every "keeper test fail" who stays costs you:
→ The A-player they're preventing you from hiring
→ The decline in team morale from watching mediocrity tolerated
→ The signal to your best people that standards are flexible

## THE FEEDBACK CULTURE

Netflix doesn't do annual reviews.
They do continuous, real-time 360 feedback.

THE 4A FEEDBACK FRAMEWORK:
Aim to Assist (give it to help, not to judge)
Actionable (specific behavior they can change)
Appreciate (receive it — don't get defensive)
Accept or Discard (decide what to act on — ultimate choice is yours)

THE "START/STOP/CONTINUE" EXERCISE:
Each person tells each colleague:
→ What should they START doing?
→ What should they STOP doing?
→ What should they CONTINUE doing?

Done quarterly. In writing. Shared with the whole team.
Sounds terrifying. Creates extraordinary growth.

## THE FREEDOM AND RESPONSIBILITY FRAMEWORK

```
LOW TALENT DENSITY + HIGH RULES = Bureaucracy
HIGH TALENT DENSITY + HIGH RULES = Frustrated talent
HIGH TALENT DENSITY + FREEDOM = Netflix model

THE EXPENSE POLICY:
Most companies: Complex expense policy, receipts required, approvals needed
Netflix: "Act in Netflix's best interest."
That's the entire policy.
Works because they only hire people with good judgment.

THE VACATION POLICY:
Most companies: X days per year, track and approve
Netflix: No vacation policy.
You decide how much time you need.
Works because managers and teams work it out together.

THE RULES THAT REMAIN:
→ Financial controls (SOX compliance)
→ Safety regulations
→ Anti-harassment
Netflix isn't anarchic. It's permission-free within clear ethical rails.
```
