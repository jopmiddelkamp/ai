# BOOK 47: MAN'S SEARCH FOR MEANING

# ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

## THE ONE LINE
The last of human freedoms is the ability to choose your attitude toward any given set of circumstances.

## LOGOTHERAPY — THE BUSINESS APPLICATION

```
Frankl survived Auschwitz. His framework for survival:
→ Meaning can be found in any situation
→ The search for meaning is the primary human motivation
→ Everything can be taken except the choice of response

BUSINESS APPLICATION:
The entrepreneurs who burn out have lost their WHY.
The entrepreneurs who sustain themselves have found meaning beyond profit.

MEANING SOURCES (from Frankl):
1. WORK: Creating something through deeds, accomplishments
2. LOVE: Experiencing someone or something (deep relationships)
3. SUFFERING: The attitude we take toward unavoidable suffering

THE MEANING QUESTION FOR ENTREPRENEURS:
"Beyond the revenue and the success — why does this matter?"
"If this business achieved everything I want — what would that enable?"
"Who benefits from my work beyond myself?"

THE WILL TO MEANING:
When work feels meaningless: Performance drops.
When work connects to purpose: Performance elevates.
Compensation for meaning is not money — it's impact.
```

## THE EXISTENTIAL VACUUM

Frankl's term for the modern condition:
Too much freedom without direction.
Too many choices without meaning.
Result: Depression, anxiety, addiction, workaholism.

THE BUSINESS SYMPTOM:
Founder achieves their financial goal. Feels empty.
"Is this all there is?"
→ Classic existential vacuum
→ The goal was never the goal
→ The journey was the meaning all along

THE FIX:
Before setting the next financial goal: Set a MEANING goal.
"What impact do I want to have by the time I achieve X?"
The impact goal gives the financial goal its meaning.
