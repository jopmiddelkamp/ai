## THE ONE LINE
Investing doesn't have to be complicated — index funds + time + consistency = wealth.

## THE WEALTH BUILDING SYSTEM

```
STEP 1: SPEND LESS THAN YOU EARN
This is the foundation. Everything else depends on it.
No investment strategy works without a savings surplus.

STEP 2: INVEST THE SURPLUS IN INDEX FUNDS
The data: 90%+ of professional fund managers underperform index funds over 20 years.
Why: Fees, trading costs, and behavioral mistakes.
The simple solution: Total Stock Market Index Fund (VTSAX or equivalent)
Cost: 0.03% annual fee vs. 1-2% for actively managed funds
Over 30 years: The fee difference compounds dramatically.

STEP 3: STAY THE COURSE
When the market crashes 30-50%: Don't sell. Buy more.
This is the hardest part. This is the ONLY part that matters.

THE MATH (rough, not guaranteed):
$1,000/month invested for 30 years at 7% average return = $1.2M
$2,000/month invested for 30 years at 7% = $2.4M
$5,000/month invested for 30 years at 7% = $6.1M

THE ONLY VARIABLES:
1. How much you save (you control)
2. How long you invest (you partially control)
3. Market returns (you don't control)

Focus on #1 and #2. Accept #3.
```

## THE F-YOU MONEY CONCEPT

```
A sum of money large enough that you could tell anyone "no"
without financial consequence.

This is not about being rude.
It's about having the freedom to make decisions based on your values,
not your financial necessity.

THE F-YOU MONEY THRESHOLD:
Different for everyone. Depends on your lifestyle cost.
Roughly: 25x your annual expenses (the 4% rule)

At this point: Your investment returns cover your expenses.
You don't NEED income. Everything you earn is a choice.

THE BUSINESS IMPLICATION:
Having F-You money changes how you negotiate.
You can walk away from bad clients.
You can turn down projects that don't align with your values.
You can take risks because failure doesn't mean poverty.

BUILD IT AGGRESSIVELY EARLY.
It compounds. And it frees you.
```
