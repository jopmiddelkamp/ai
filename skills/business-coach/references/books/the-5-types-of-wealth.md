## THE WEALTH AUDIT TOOL

```
Rate yourself 1-10 on each type. Be honest.

TIME WEALTH: ___ /10
→ How many hours per week do you control completely?
→ Can you take a 2-week vacation without crisis?
→ Do you have time for the things that matter most?

SOCIAL WEALTH: ___ /10
→ Do you have 3+ people you could call at 2am in a crisis?
→ Are your most important relationships thriving?
→ Do you feel genuinely connected, not just networked?

MENTAL WEALTH: ___ /10
→ How often do you feel anxious vs. at peace?
→ Do you have a clear sense of purpose?
→ Are you learning and growing regularly?

PHYSICAL WEALTH: ___ /10
→ Do you have energy throughout the day?
→ Are you sleeping 7-8 hours consistently?
→ Are you moving your body regularly?
→ Are you eating in a way that serves you?

FINANCIAL WEALTH: ___ /10
→ Is your passive income > your expenses?
→ Are you building assets?
→ Do you have 6+ months cash buffer?
→ Are you on track to your financial goals?

YOUR TOTAL: ___ /50

HIGHEST SCORE: Your strength. Protect it.
LOWEST SCORE: Your bottleneck. Focus here.

THE REBALANCING QUESTION:
"Am I sacrificing Type ___ to accumulate Type ___?
Is that trade worth it right now?"
```

## THE COMPOUNDING WEALTH INSIGHTS

Each type of wealth compounds differently:
→ TIME: Compounds through habits and systems
→ SOCIAL: Compounds through decades of relationship investment
→ MENTAL: Compounds through consistent learning and reflection
→ PHYSICAL: Compounds through daily small choices (hardest to rebuild)
→ FINANCIAL: Compounds through returns on invested capital

The tragedy: Most people focus entirely on Financial, which compounds most visibly.
They neglect Social and Physical, which take longest to rebuild when lost.
