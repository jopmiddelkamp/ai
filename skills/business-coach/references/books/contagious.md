## THE ONE LINE
Ideas, products, and behaviors spread because of STEPPS — not because of advertising.

## THE STEPPS FRAMEWORK — APPLIED

```
S — SOCIAL CURRENCY
People share things that make them look good.
The question: Does sharing your content make the person look smart? 
              Interesting? In-the-know? Ahead of the curve?

TACTICS:
→ Make them feel like insiders ("Most people don't know this...")
→ Make scarcity visible ("Only for our community...")
→ Create game mechanics (streaks, levels, badges)
→ Make the remarkable visible (Snapple facts, unusual products)

FOR LEADWITHZOE:
→ "The AI secret most working moms don't know" = social currency
→ Community of "Claude moms" = insider status

T — TRIGGERS
What environmental cues remind people of your product?
The more often they're triggered, the more they share.

PEANUT BUTTER triggers JELLY (Kit Kat + coffee)
MONDAY triggers the "I hate Mondays" content
MORNING ROUTINE triggers productivity content

DESIGN YOUR TRIGGER:
What daily moment should trigger your audience to think of you?
→ Morning coffee → "I use Claude during my morning routine"
→ Kids' homework time → "Claude helps me help my kids"
→ Sunday planning → "Claude plans my week"

E — EMOTION
High arousal emotions spread. Low arousal don't.

HIGH AROUSAL (spreads): Awe, excitement, anger, anxiety
LOW AROUSAL (doesn't spread): Contentment, sadness, relaxation

Your content should make people feel SOMETHING intensely.
Not warm and fuzzy. INTENSELY.

FOR CONTENT CREATORS:
Awe = "I can't believe this is possible" (viral potential)
Excitement = "I have to try this right now" (shares)
Anxiety = "I'm behind on this" (saves)
Anger = "This is unfair" (comments and shares, but risky)

P — PUBLIC
If people can see others using something, they're more likely to use it.

"Live Laugh Love" signs spread because you can see them in homes.
iPhone's white earbuds spread because you could see them everywhere.

MAKE YOUR PRODUCT VISIBLE:
→ "Made with Claude" watermark on outputs
→ Sharing screenshots of Claude conversations
→ Tagging in posts when using AI tools

P — PRACTICAL VALUE
People share useful information. "News you can use."

The email you forward contains useful advice or information.
The article you share solves a real problem.
The video you repost shows how to do something.

PRACTICAL VALUE CONTENT TYPES:
→ How-to (step by step)
→ Templates and frameworks
→ "I didn't know this" discoveries
→ Lists that save time

S — STORIES
People think in stories. Information wrapped in narrative spreads better than facts alone.

THE TROJAN HORSE:
Your message should be embedded in a story that can't be told without it.
Not "here's the feature" — "here's what happened when I used it."
The story carries the message. The message can't be extracted from the story.

FOR LEADWITHZOE:
Every reel is a story: "Here's what happened when Claude..."
The product (Claude insight) can't be extracted from the story.
So the whole story spreads.
```
