## THE CRUCIAL CONVERSATION SCRIPTS

**WHEN SOMEONE MISSES A COMMITMENT:**
"Last Tuesday we agreed you'd have the proposal to the client by Friday.
It didn't go out. Help me understand what happened."
[Listen]
"Going forward, what's the system that prevents this from happening?"

**WHEN SOMEONE'S PERFORMANCE IS DECLINING:**
"I've noticed over the past month that [specific observable facts].
I care about you and I care about the team, so I want to address this directly.
What's going on?"
[Listen with genuine curiosity]
"What support do you need from me? And what do you commit to changing?"

**WHEN THERE'S A CULTURAL VIOLATION:**
"What happened is important to address.
[Describe the situation]
That behavior is inconsistent with who we are as a team.
[State the core value it violated]
What needs to happen for this not to occur again?"

**WHEN GIVING DIFFICULT FEEDBACK TO A PEER:**
"Can I share something I've noticed? I want to bring it up because I respect you.
[Describe the behavior — facts only]
The impact I've observed is [specific impact].
I might be missing context. What's your perspective?"

## THE SILENCE-VIOLENCE SPECTRUM

```
←─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────→
SILENCE                                              VIOLENCE
Masking      Avoiding      Withdrawing    Controlling    Labeling    Attacking

Most people oscillate between silence and violence.
Neither works. Both destroy dialogue.

Target: The middle. Honest, caring, direct dialogue.
The path to the middle runs through MUTUAL SAFETY.
```
