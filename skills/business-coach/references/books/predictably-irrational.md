## THE ONE LINE
Human irrationality is systematic and predictable — understanding the patterns lets you design better products and make better decisions.

## THE MOST BUSINESS-RELEVANT FINDINGS

```
1. THE POWER OF FREE
"Free" changes behavior disproportionately.
The difference between $0 and $0.01 is far larger 
than the difference between $0.01 and $0.02.

BUSINESS APPLICATION:
→ Free trials convert better than discounted trials
→ Free shipping converts better than cheap shipping
→ "Free bonus" in an offer is disproportionately powerful

2. ARBITRARY COHERENCE
First price you see becomes your anchor for all future prices.
Even random numbers anchor your willingness to pay.

BUSINESS APPLICATION:
→ Always show highest price option first
→ Create a premium offering even if few buy it (anchors others)
→ Display original price before sale price (always)

3. SOCIAL NORMS vs. MARKET NORMS
When you introduce money into a social interaction:
market norms replace social norms.
And market norms are much weaker motivators for most people.

EXAMPLE: Lawyers who donate hours have high participation.
If you offer them $30/hour: participation drops dramatically.
The small payment transforms a social good into a bad market deal.

BUSINESS APPLICATION:
→ Employee culture: Don't let everything be transactional
→ Customer community: Don't monetize everything
→ Some relationships should remain social, not market-based

4. THE EFFECT OF EXPECTATIONS
If you believe something will be good: it often is.
Beer study: Told it has vinegar = worse. Not told = tastes great.
Same beer.

BUSINESS APPLICATION:
→ Presentation affects perceived quality
→ Pricing signals quality (premium price = premium experience)
→ The unboxing experience affects product rating
→ First impressions disproportionately determine satisfaction

5. THE HIGH COST OF OWNERSHIP
People overvalue things they own.
IKEA effect: You value what you build.
Endowment effect: You value what you have.

BUSINESS APPLICATION:
→ Free trials work: After using it, they value it more
→ Customization increases loyalty (they built it with you)
→ Getting customers invested early (setting up their profile) = more loyalty
```
