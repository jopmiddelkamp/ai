# BOOK 94: DIE EMPTY (Todd Henry)

```
THE ONE LINE: Don't go to your grave with your best work still inside you.

THE BRILLIANT REGRET:
Most people die with their best work unfinished.
The book they were going to write.
The business they were going to start.
The relationship they were going to repair.

Not from lack of ability.
From lack of urgency.
"I'll do it someday."

THE URGENCY PRACTICE:
Treat your most important work as perishable.
If you don't do it today: It may never happen.
Not because you'll die (maybe) — because the moment passes.
Your ideas are most alive right now.
Tomorrow they'll be a little dimmer.

THE 5 ENEMIES OF CREATIVITY:
1. AIMLESSNESS: No clear direction → Busy but not productive
2. BOREDOM: Comfort ≠ Fulfillment → Playing small
3. FEAR: Safe choices over meaningful ones
4. EXPECTATION: Pressure to perform → Creativity shrinks
5. COMFORT: The greatest thief of unlived potential

THE DAILY QUESTION:
"Am I working on what matters most today?
Or am I managing the urgent at the expense of the important?"
```
