# 🏗️ STAGE 04: SCALING STAGE ($10M+)
### When the Business Becomes an Institution

## THE SCALING STAGE SHIFT

```
WHAT CHANGES AT $10M:
→ Founder can no longer know everyone personally
→ Culture must be institutionalized (not just "felt")
→ Systems must run without founder input
→ Management of managers becomes the primary skill
→ Capital allocation becomes as important as sales strategy
→ Competitive dynamics intensify (you're visible now)

THE FOUNDER ROLE TRANSITION:
$1M: Founder is the business
$5M: Founder leads the team
$10M: Founder leads the leaders
$25M: Founder sets vision, leaders run the business
$50M+: Founder can step back or become executive chairman
```

## THE SCALING OPERATING SYSTEM

```
FROM SCALING UP (HARNISH):

THE ONE PERSON WHO RUNS THE BUSINESS:
Identify your integrator/COO.
They manage day-to-day. You manage them and strategy.
Weekly 1:1. Clear decision authority.

THE LEADERSHIP TEAM STRUCTURE:
CEO → Integrator/COO → [Sales, Marketing, Operations, Finance, Product]

Each function has:
→ A single accountable owner
→ Clear measurable targets
→ Quarterly rocks
→ A department scorecard

THE QUARTERLY PLANNING PROCESS:
2-day offsite. Leadership team only.
→ Review last quarter's performance
→ Assess company health (people, strategy, execution, cash)
→ Set next quarter's company and individual rocks
→ Identify and solve top 3 strategic issues

THE TOWN HALL:
Quarterly all-hands.
→ Review company progress against annual plan
→ Share strategic context employees need
→ Celebrate wins
→ Address concerns directly
```

## THE SCALING CULTURE CHALLENGE

```
THE CULTURE DILUTION PROBLEM:
At 10 people: Culture is felt.
At 50 people: Culture must be taught.
At 200 people: Culture must be systematized.

THE SOLUTIONS:

HIRING FOR CULTURE AT SCALE:
→ Structured interview questions that reveal values alignment
→ Culture interviews separate from skills interviews
→ Multiple interviewers assessing the same dimensions
→ Reference checks focused on cultural fit, not just performance

ONBOARDING FOR CULTURE:
→ Culture-focused first day (not just logistics)
→ Assign a culture buddy (not just a skills buddy)
→ Stories told in onboarding (not just values listed)
→ 30/60/90 day check-ins on culture fit (not just performance)

PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT FOR CULTURE:
→ Culture dimensions on performance reviews
→ Culture violations as serious as performance violations
→ Leaders evaluated on culture they create (not just results)
```
