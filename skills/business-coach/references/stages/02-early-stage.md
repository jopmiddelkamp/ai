# 🏗️ STAGE 02: EARLY STAGE ($0 to $1M)
### From First Sale to First Million

## THE EARLY STAGE REALITY

```
What changes from Idea Stage:
→ You have a paying customer (validation achieved)
→ The question shifts from "will it work?" to "how do I grow it?"

What stays the same:
→ Cash is everything
→ Every dollar counts
→ You can't afford to be wrong about major decisions

The #1 early stage mistake: Premature scaling
Building the team, office, or infrastructure before you've proven
the model consistently works.
```

## THE 5 EARLY STAGE PRIORITIES IN ORDER

**PRIORITY 1: NAIL YOUR REPEATABLE SALES PROCESS**
```
Before you hire a salesperson: Know how to sell it yourself.
Document: What do you say? What questions do you ask?
          What objections come up? How do you handle them?
          At what point do they say yes?

The repeatable sales process:
1. Lead qualification (does this person have the problem?)
2. Discovery (understand their specific situation)
3. Presentation (show relevant solution)
4. Objection handling (address their concerns)
5. Close (ask for the sale clearly)

Until you can teach this: Don't hire sales.
```

**PRIORITY 2: DELIVER SO WELL YOUR FIRST 10 CUSTOMERS BECOME YOUR SALES TEAM**
```
Your first 10 customers:
→ Will refer others if you over-deliver
→ Will warn others if you under-deliver
→ Are your most important marketing assets

OVER-DELIVERY TACTICS:
→ Check in more than they expect
→ Give them something they didn't ask for
→ Introduce them to someone relevant
→ Solve a problem adjacent to the one you were hired for
→ Make them feel like your most important client (they are)
```

**PRIORITY 3: MANAGE CASH LIKE YOUR LIFE DEPENDS ON IT**
```
Early stage companies die from cash problems, not idea problems.

CASH RULES:
→ Know your runway (months until you run out)
→ Know your monthly burn rate
→ Know your break-even point
→ Invoice immediately. Follow up on late payments immediately.
→ Pay yourself last (if you pay yourself at all)

THE REAL-TIME DASHBOARD:
Cash in bank: ___
Monthly burn: ___
Runway: ___ months
Monthly revenue needed to break even: ___
```

**PRIORITY 4: FIND YOUR ONE GROWTH CHANNEL**
```
Don't try to do everything. One channel, mastered.

THE CHANNEL DECISION FOR $0-$1M:
→ Do you have existing network? → Referrals/relationships
→ Are you good on camera? → Video content
→ Are you good at writing? → Content marketing
→ Do you have budget? → Paid acquisition (test carefully)
→ Are you a strong salesperson? → Outbound

PICK ONE. Master it. Build all $1M on that foundation.
Add second channel only after first is working without you.
```

**PRIORITY 5: BUILD YOUR FIRST SYSTEM BEFORE YOU HIRE**
```
The mistake: Hire someone to "figure it out"
The right move: Document the process, then hire to follow the process

FOR YOUR FIRST HIRE:
Document every task the role requires.
Estimate time per task.
Define what "done right" looks like for each.
Hire to that specification.
Train against your documentation.
```
