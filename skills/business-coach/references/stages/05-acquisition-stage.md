# 🏗️ STAGE 05: ACQUISITION STAGE
### Buying Your Way to Scale

## THE ACQUISITION DECISION FRAMEWORK

```
WHY ACQUIRE INSTEAD OF BUILD:
→ Buy 3-5 years of growth instantly
→ Buy proven customers, revenue, team
→ Buy market position (harder to build than buy)
→ Buy capabilities you don't have

WHEN TO ACQUIRE:
→ You have operational excellence you can apply to new business
→ You have capital or can raise it
→ You understand the target industry
→ The target has something you can improve (operational leverage)

WHEN NOT TO ACQUIRE:
→ Your own house is not in order
→ You're buying to avoid fixing your own business
→ You don't understand the business you're buying
→ The valuation requires things to go perfectly
```

## THE ACQUISITION PROCESS (From Main Street Millionaire)

```
PHASE 1: SOURCING (1-3 months)
→ Define your target criteria (industry, size, geography, SDE range)
→ Sources: Brokers, BizBuySell, direct outreach, industry associations
→ Build relationships with brokers in your target niche
→ Evaluate 20-30 opportunities for every 1 you pursue seriously

PHASE 2: INITIAL EVALUATION (2-4 weeks per target)
→ Review CIM (Confidential Information Memorandum)
→ Financial screening: SDE, multiple, growth trend
→ Red flags: Customer concentration, declining revenue, owner dependence
→ Letter of Intent if passes screening

PHASE 3: DUE DILIGENCE (30-60 days)
→ Financial (3 years tax returns, bank statements, A/R aging)
→ Legal (contracts, leases, litigation, IP)
→ Operational (key employees, processes, systems)
→ Customer (retention, satisfaction, concentration)
→ Hire: CPA for financials, attorney for legal, industry expert if needed

PHASE 4: DEAL STRUCTURE (2-4 weeks)
→ Asset sale vs. stock sale (usually asset sale for buyer protection)
→ Financing: Seller financing + SBA + equity
→ Transition period: 30-90 days of seller involvement
→ Earnout structure if revenue claims need validation
→ Non-compete agreement

PHASE 5: CLOSE AND TRANSITION
→ Day 1-30: Listen. Don't change anything yet.
→ Day 31-60: Identify quick wins and culture assessment
→ Day 61-90: Install systems, begin improvements
→ Month 4-6: Full operational control
```
